Demand, capital and inventory

210 historical M5 item-store series link probabilistic forecasts to replenishment under common capital/capacity constraints. Fifteen final weekly origins and overlapping horizons limit inference. Operating effects are simulations, not observed retailer savings.

Indian inflation release vintages

48 official releases and 144 vintage observations. An 18-release final check retains persistence: RMSE 0.8185 pp versus 1.6901 for fixed AR(1). Release dates and the later base revision remain separate. No current inflation forecast is claimed.
