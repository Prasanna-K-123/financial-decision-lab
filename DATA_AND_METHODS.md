# Definitions and data rights

All main monetary inputs are USD millions as reported, not translated from an
assumed domestic currency. Microsoft's fiscal end is 30June, Infosys 31March;
Target FY 2023/24/25 ends 3February2024,1February2025,31January2026. Target FY 2023
contains 53 weeks. Net income and equity are consolidated, including NCI where
present. Cash excludes investment balances.

The twelve extraction targets and three controls are fixed in PROTOCOL.md.
A supported financial answer requires the exact requested fiscal flow/instant,
reporting unit, currency, consolidation and a valid source locator. Microsoft
combined D&A/other, Infosys combined PPE/intangibles and Target's absent direct
total-liabilities line are not silently substituted for pure/direct targets.
Annotations are workflow transcriptions, not external human review.

Original annual reports belong to their issuers and remain in the private
source cache. Public files contain numerical research facts, short common row
labels, original-file hashes and locators, plus issuer links. Publication does
not grant redistribution rights in issuer reports, logos, or artwork. No
third-party raw source is included in the repository or Pages exports.

The code, numeric research, documents and workbook are project work attributed
to Prasanna K. No third-party report is covered by an inferred code licence.

The future model is an operating-policy scenario anchored to reported EBIT.
It uses assumed normalised tax and aggregate net growth investment. Historical
CFO-minus-cash-PPE is not used as an FCFF bridge. Reported expense/lease basis
is retained; no harmonised marginal ROIC estimate or enterprise-to-equity
bridge is asserted. No quoted price or current market calibration is used.
